About

I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology (RSET), Kakkanad, where I have taught since October 2020. Before that I taught engineering, science and computer applications students at colleges across Kerala, and led the Department of Computer Applications at KMM College of Arts and Science for several years.

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics under APJ Abdul Kalam Kerala Technological University, and I hold the State Eligibility Test (SET) qualification. Outside the classroom I enjoy travelling and listening to music.

Research

Ph.D. in progress · registered 9 August 2016

Fuzzy Mathematical Morphology

Doctoral research at RSET under APJ Abdul Kalam Kerala Technological University. The work applies morphological operators such as dilation and erosion to fuzzy graph models, and studies the lattice and topological structure that results.

Intuitionistic fuzzy graphs

Morphological operators, topological spaces and applications.

Pythagorean fuzzy graphs

Lattice structures of dilations and erosions.

Neutrosophic graphs

Directed neutrosophic graphs processed with morphological operators.
